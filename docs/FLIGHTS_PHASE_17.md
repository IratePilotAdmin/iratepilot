## Purpose

Phase 17 extends the protected `/admin/flights` workspace with a static, candidate-neutral execution-control boundary for a future supplier-evidence review. It defines how an actual Phase 15 authorization, separately approved Phase 16 preflight receipt, fixed evidence inventory, immutable hashes and lineage, frozen rubric, independent roles, isolated access, one-criterion-at-a-time review, sanitized work product, calculation references, variance, dissent, findings, stops, expiry, closeout, and no-downstream-authority controls would have to be bound.

Phase 16 software is repository-verified, Git-published, deployed, and authenticated browser-accepted in isolated Preview. That proves the protected software surface works; it does not create actual Phase 15 authorization, a Phase 16 preflight receipt, evidence, a rubric, reviewer assignments, access, an action-time start decision, or review-opening authority. None of those prerequisites exists, so Phase 17 execution control remains blocked and evidence review remains closed.

## Seven controlled review stages

1. Authorization, preflight, and scope binding.
2. Fixed inventory and rubric release.
3. Reviewer, observer, and access release.
4. One-criterion-at-a-time review.
5. Variance, dissent, finding, and stop record.
6. Sanitized work-product and audit record.
7. Expiry, closeout, and no-recommendation record.

These are static design controls. They do not create authorization, preflight, a receipt, evidence, hashes, lineage, a rubric, an identity, an assignment, access, a review session, an observation, a calculation, a score, a scorecard, a finding, a stop record, a closeout, a recommendation, a shortlist, a contract, a supplier selection, or an external capability.

## Five immediate-stop safeguards

1. No-implicit-review-opening lock.
2. Inventory, rubric, and lineage immutability lock.
3. Independent-role, access, and observer-veto lock.
4. Evidence, work-product, variance, and abort lock.
5. Review-closeout and no-downstream-authority lock.

Missing, stale, expired, broadened, disputed, inferred, drifted, conflicted, overprivileged, irreproducible, contaminated, suppressed, unresolved, or incomplete prerequisite or execution evidence keeps both execution control and review blocked.

## Ten separately owned gates

1. Phase 15 authorization separately satisfied.
2. Phase 16 preflight separately approved.
3. One-time review scope, inventory, and window bound.
4. Inventory, hashes, lineage, and admissibility reverified.
5. Rubric version, weights, thresholds, and calculations frozen.
6. Reviewers, observer, conflicts, recusals, and access verified.
7. Isolation, prohibited-data, work-product, and incident controls verified.
8. Criterion sequence, variance, dissent, finding, and stop controls verified.
9. Expiry, closeout, and no recommendation, selection, or release verified.

Every gate starts incomplete. Completing the design cannot create the evidence needed by a gate, validate a prerequisite, open a review, calculate a score, or create standing authority.
