# Hotel-manager private-pilot recruitment packet

Prepared: August 16, 2026

Status: Meeting-ready; no manager account, invitation, property, or transaction is authorized by this packet

Use this packet to qualify an owner, general manager, revenue manager, or sales manager for a supervised iRatePilot hotel pilot. The meeting may happen before commercial launch. It must not be presented as approval for a public listing, guaranteed reservations, live payments, payouts, supplier traffic, or SynXis connectivity.

## Pilot proposition

iRatePilot gives an authorized hotel team a controlled workspace to prepare a private property listing, room types, rates, and future inventory. During the pilot:

- the hotel remains private until separate administrator review and publication approval;
- delegated managers may work only on authorized inactive property content, rooms, rates, and future inventory;
- managers cannot publish a hotel, transfer a property, delete rooms or inventory, invite teammates, access billing or payouts, or enable supplier traffic;
- test data and Stripe test mode are used unless a later written gate explicitly authorizes commercial activation; and
- iRatePilot does not request hotel passwords, PMS credentials, API keys, bank credentials, card data, or identity documents in the recruitment meeting.

## Ideal first-pilot profile

Prefer a property that has:

1. A verified owner or authorized general, revenue, or sales manager willing to participate.
2. A clear legal business and property identity with an official website.
3. Written permission to use approved property content, photos, room details, policies, rates, and limited pilot inventory.
4. At least one room type and a small future date range suitable for supervised validation.
5. A named operating contact and backup contact for reconciliation and support.
6. A short approval path, with any ownership, management-company, franchise, brand, legal, or technology approvals identified.
7. Agreement that the initial stage is private and does not create guaranteed reservations or live money movement.

Stop qualification if identity, authority, content rights, rate ownership, or the intended payout entity cannot be verified.

| Time | Topic | Outcome |

## Core qualification questions

1. Are you authorized to approve a private booking-technology pilot for this property?
2. If not, who must approve it: the owner, management company, franchise, brand, legal team, or technology team?
3. May iRatePilot use the hotel's approved name, descriptions, photos, room information, policies, rates, and limited pilot inventory?
4. Which PMS, CRS, channel manager, or manual inventory process does the hotel use? Do not request credentials.
5. Who owns rate and inventory decisions for the property?
6. Can the hotel provide at least one room type, occupancy, base rate, taxes, fees, cancellation terms, and a small future date range?
7. Who will verify that the private listing and inventory are accurate?
8. Who will support a supervised test booking, cancellation/refund scenario, messaging test, and reconciliation review?
9. Are there required commercial, commission, privacy, accessibility, seller-of-travel, or brand approvals?
10. Does the hotel agree that publication, manager invitation, Stripe Connect onboarding, live booking, payout, and supplier activation each require a later approval?

## Safe meeting language

Use:

> We are preparing a controlled private pilot. Your authorized manager can build and review a hotel draft, rooms, rates, and limited future inventory. Nothing is published and no live payment, payout, or supplier traffic is enabled without separate review and approval.

Do not promise:

- a launch date before the exit gates pass;
- booking volume, revenue, occupancy, rate improvement, or placement;
- automatic PMS/CRS connectivity;
- live customer payment or hotel payout availability; or
- publication based only on meeting attendance or form completion.

## Meeting outcomes

- **Qualified for intake:** verified decision-maker, property fit, authority path, pilot scope, and operating contact are clear.
- **Referral required:** participant must introduce the authorized owner, manager, or approver.
- **Additional approval required:** ownership, management-company, franchise, brand, legal, or technology approval is pending.
- **Follow-up required:** interest exists, but required property or operating information is incomplete.
- **Declined or not qualified:** authority, rights, fit, or safeguards are not acceptable.

## Next steps after a qualified meeting

1. Complete the [meeting checklist](./MEETING_CHECKLIST.md).
2. Complete the [first-pilot acceptance form](./FIRST_PILOT_ACCEPTANCE_FORM.md) without secrets or bank information.
3. If the representative is qualified and authorized, let them submit the verified isolated Preview [hotel-manager intake](https://project-w1cin.vercel.app/hotel-intake) from their own device or a supervised browser.
4. Stop at the receipt page. Submission creates a pending intake only and does not approve or publish the hotel.
5. Independently verify the property, business, participant, authority, and content rights.
6. Review the pending record in the Preview admin queue and compare it with the packet.
7. Review the packet against the [first-hotel onboarding runbook](../FIRST_HOTEL_ONBOARDING_RUNBOOK.md).
8. Request a separate approval before creating a partner record, inactive property draft, or manager invitation.
9. Keep the property inactive through private content and inventory review.
10. Use separate gates for Stripe Connect, publication, controlled live booking, supplier traffic, and SynXis.

After a verified partner account and inactive property are separately approved, use the [partner portal acceptance checklist](./PARTNER_PORTAL_ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md) for the supervised operator-experience session. An administrator walkthrough or synthetic fixture cannot close that external acceptance gate.
