## Before the meeting

- [ ] The property name, official website, public business address, and business telephone were independently located.
- [ ] The participant's business role or referral path can be verified using a hotel-controlled or company-controlled source.
- [ ] The private-pilot limitations and prohibited data list are ready to explain.
- [ ] No live booking, payment, payout, supplier, or SynXis action is scheduled.
- [ ] No manager account or invitation has been created for this meeting.

## Authority and approvals

- [ ] Participant is the owner, general manager, revenue manager, or sales manager.
- [ ] Participant states whether they can approve the pilot.
- [ ] Written authority is available or the authorized approver has been identified.
- [ ] Ownership or management-company approval requirement is recorded.
- [ ] Franchise or brand approval requirement is recorded.
- [ ] Legal, privacy, accessibility, marketing, or technology approval requirement is recorded.
- [ ] Rights to use property content, photos, room details, policies, rates, and pilot inventory are confirmed or marked pending.

## Property and operating fit

- [ ] Property type and star rating are recorded.
- [ ] Official property address, country, website, and public telephone are recorded.
- [ ] PMS, CRS, channel manager, or manual inventory method is identified without credentials.
- [ ] Rate and inventory owner is identified.
- [ ] At least one room type and maximum occupancy can be supplied.
- [ ] Base rate, taxes, fees, cancellation terms, and future pilot dates can be supplied.
- [ ] Approved public HTTPS photo and at least one amenity can be supplied.
- [ ] Primary operating contact is identified.
- [ ] Backup or escalation contact is identified.

## Pilot boundaries acknowledged

- [ ] Property begins as an inactive private draft.
- [ ] Manager access is limited to authorized inactive content, rooms, rates, and future inventory.
- [ ] Manager cannot publish, transfer the property, delete rooms/inventory, invite teammates, or access billing/payouts.
- [ ] Initial validation uses test or synthetic transactions only.
- [ ] Publication, Stripe Connect, live booking, payouts, supplier traffic, and SynXis require separate approvals.
- [ ] No booking volume, revenue, placement, or launch-date guarantee was made.

## Prohibited information check

Confirm none of the following was requested, received, copied, photographed, or stored:

- [ ] Passwords, one-time codes, API keys, access tokens, or webhook secrets
- [ ] PMS, CRS, email, or bank login credentials
- [ ] Card numbers or bank account/routing numbers
- [ ] Tax IDs, passports, driver's licenses, or other identity documents
- [ ] Production customer lists or unnecessary guest personal information

If any prohibited information was offered, record only that it was rejected or safely removed; do not reproduce it:
