## Entry requirements

- [ ] Participant identity, business role, authority, and hotel relationship were independently verified.
- [ ] Participant signed in with their own account; no credentials were shared with the operator.
- [ ] The session is on the Preview alias, not Production.
- [ ] The assigned property is inactive and belongs to the participant's authorized organization.
- [ ] Public booking, live payments, payouts, publication, and supplier traffic remain disabled.
- [ ] No invitation, Stripe Connect onboarding, or external-email action is approved for this session.

Stop if any entry requirement fails.

## Restriction confirmation

Confirm the participant cannot or does not attempt to:

- [ ] Publish or activate the hotel.
- [ ] Transfer a property or change its organization.
- [ ] Delete rooms or inventory when acting as a delegated manager.
- [ ] Invite teammates or change owner-only access.
- [ ] Access or activate live billing, payouts, Stripe Connect, PMS, CRS, or supplier traffic.
- [ ] View another partner's property, reservations, finance, messages, or integrations.

## Exit check

- [ ] No hotel was published.
- [ ] No live booking, payment, refund, transfer, or payout was created.
- [ ] No manager invitation or transactional email was sent.
- [ ] No Stripe Connect, PMS, CRS, supplier, or SynXis action was started.
