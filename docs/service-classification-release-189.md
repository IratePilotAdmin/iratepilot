# Service reconciliation after category changes

Migration 189 includes effective opening classifications in the service-pricing fingerprint. Previously approved allocations become outdated when those classifications change. When every opening reversal is classified, category totals reflect effective accommodation, tax, hotel fee and OTA fee reductions; separate folio charges and their targeted reversals supply other revenue. New allocation approval must preserve those totals. Classification inserts and revisions flag previously recorded service revenue for reconciliation.

Local hotel and home tests cover category revision, outdated allocation rejection, a zero-total forward adjustment, service close, balanced journal posting, trial balance reconciliation, accounting-period closure, and journal retry without further writes. The original service-entry fixture is inserted directly; subsequent approval, close and posting steps use the application APIs. This is not a browser or real-property acceptance test.

Named tax buckets and individual fee categories still require stronger end-to-end enforcement. Current fixed totals distinguish aggregate tax and hotel fees; they do not prove preservation of every city/state/lodging or resort/technology/cleaning allocation. No hosted installation has occurred. This release is not sufficient to declare accounting or hotel launch complete.
