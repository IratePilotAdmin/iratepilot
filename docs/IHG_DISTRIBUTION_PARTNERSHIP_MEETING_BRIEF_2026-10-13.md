## Approval and authority questions

1. Which IHG entity and team can approve a new B2C OTA or distribution partner?
2. Does approval cover the IHG system, individual brands, participating franchisees, or each property separately?
3. May a franchise owner sponsor or join a pilot, or must corporate approval come first?
4. What application, due-diligence, financial, insurance, licensing, security, and legal documents are required?
5. Are there minimum booking volume, market coverage, accreditation, operating-history, or funding requirements?
6. Which agreement governs distribution, content rights, brand use, data processing, support, and termination?
7. What written evidence will show that iRatePilot is authorized to proceed to technical onboarding?

## Inventory and reservation questions

1. What is the approved connectivity route for rates, availability, restrictions, room content, reservations, modifications, and cancellations?
2. Is the connection direct with IHG, through an approved distribution provider, or through a property-authorized PMS or channel manager?
3. Which documented APIs, message formats, authentication methods, webhooks, and certification environments apply?
4. Does IHG provide a sandbox and test properties? What certification cases must pass?
5. How are property identifiers, room types, rate plans, taxes, mandatory fees, cancellation rules, and loyalty-eligible rates mapped?
6. What is the required workflow for reservation acknowledgements, failures, duplicate prevention, amendments, cancellations, and no-shows?
7. What production smoke test, monitoring, support escalation, and rollback process is required before live traffic?

Do not propose a specific connector as an IHG-approved path unless Mayra confirms it in writing.

## Commercial and customer-operation questions

1. Can iRatePilot propose a **13% marketplace commission plus a mandatory 3% iRate Rewards Program contribution**, for a **16% total hotel distribution cost**, with no traveler service fee?
2. Does IHG require different economics, brand-specific terms, rate parity, closed-user-group rules, or loyalty restrictions?
3. Who is merchant of record, and who owns payment authorization, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, taxes, and hotel payouts?
4. Which party provides traveler support before, during, and after the stay?
5. What service-level, incident-notification, reconciliation, audit, fraud, privacy, and data-retention requirements apply?
6. What property photos, descriptions, trademarks, and brand assets may iRatePilot display, and through what approved source?
