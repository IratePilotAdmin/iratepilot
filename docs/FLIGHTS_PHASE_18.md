## Purpose

Phase 18 extends the protected `/admin/flights` workspace with a static, read-only authorization boundary for a possible future Duffel diligence contact. It defines how one narrow purpose, accountable organizational sender, independent approvals, immutable approved message, truthful disclosures, official channel, recipient-role validation, data minimization, one-contact limit, start and expiry, revocation, immediate stops, incident handling, minimal receipt, response disposition, closeout, and no-commitment controls would have to be bound.

The executive documentation-only provider-path preference records Duffel as the primary intended diligence and launch path and Sabre as the inactive secondary expansion path. That preference is not a recommendation, contracted supplier selection, provider-contact authorization, or external authority. No accountable sender, message, recipient, channel, action-time decision, contact window, or permission to contact Duffel exists, so Phase 18 contact authorization remains blocked.

## Six authorization artifacts

1. Provider-path and purpose record.
2. Accountable owner and authority record.
3. Approved message and disclosure packet.
4. Approved channel and recipient scope.
5. Expiry, stop, withdrawal, and incident plan.
6. Contact receipt and no-commitment closeout.

These are static design artifacts. They do not create a sender, approver, recipient, address, message, disclosure, channel, contact window, attempt, receipt, response, evidence, case, account, contract, commitment, recommendation, supplier selection, credential, or external capability.

## Five immediate-stop safeguards

1. No-implicit-contact-authority lock.
2. Named-provider, purpose, and recipient lock.
3. Message, disclosure, and data-minimization lock.
4. One-time channel, expiry, and stop lock.
5. No-commitment, intake, or downstream-authority lock.

A missing, stale, expired, broadened, delegated, altered, unverifiable, redirected, retried, forwarded, sensitive, incident-affected, commitment-bearing, or otherwise nonconforming prerequisite keeps provider contact blocked.

## Ten separately owned gates

1. Executive provider-path preference verified.
2. One contact purpose and Duffel scope bound.
3. Accountable sender and approvers named.
4. Approved message and no-commitment language frozen.
5. Required disclosures and prohibited statements verified.
6. Recipient role and official channel allowlisted.
7. Data minimization, security, and recordkeeping verified.
8. Start, expiry, revocation, stop, and incident controls bound.
9. Response handling, closeout, and no-intake boundary verified.
10. Contact requires separate action-time authorization.

Every gate starts incomplete. Completing the design cannot create the evidence needed by a gate, validate a person or recipient, approve a message or channel, open a contact window, send anything, or create standing authority.
