## Purpose

Phase 19 extends the protected `/admin/flights` workspace with a static, read-only preflight boundary for a possible future Duffel diligence contact. It defines the independent verification that would have to occur immediately before any separate one-time contact decision could be considered: an actual Phase 18 authorization reference, narrow provider and purpose scope, accountable sender and independent approvers, exact immutable message and disclosures, official recipient role and channel authenticity, data minimization, privacy, security, recordkeeping, fixed contact window, expiry, revocation, immediate stops, incident handling, no retry, response disposition, closeout, and no-downstream-release controls.

Phase 18 software was accepted in isolated Preview, but that acceptance is not actual provider-contact authorization. No actual Phase 18 authorization, authorization reference, sender, approver, message, recipient role, verified channel, preflight decision, contact window, or action-time decision exists. Phase 19 therefore remains blocked and cannot satisfy, replace, infer, or bypass Phase 18 authority.

## Seven preflight controls

1. Phase 18 authorization and scope reference.
2. Sender, approver, identity, conflict, and acknowledgment control.
3. Message, disclosure, and no-commitment freeze.
4. Recipient role, channel, domain, and authenticity verification.
5. Data minimization, privacy, security, and recordkeeping control.
6. Window, expiry, revocation, stop, incident, and no-retry control.
7. Response disposition, closeout, and no-downstream-release control.

These are static design controls. They do not create or verify authority, assign or authenticate a person, draft or approve a message, identify a recipient, validate a channel, open a window, authorize an attempt, create a receipt, admit a response, open evidence intake, or release any downstream capability.

## Five immediate-stop safeguards

1. No-implicit-preflight-or-contact-opening lock.
2. Authorization, provider, purpose, and message immutability lock.
3. Sender, recipient, channel, identity, and conflict lock.
4. Sensitive-data, attachment, credential, and commitment stop lock.
5. Expiry, revocation, incident, no-retry, and no-downstream lock.

A missing, stale, expired, altered, broadened, reused, delegated, conflicted, unverifiable, impersonated, redirected, sensitive, credential-bearing, attachment-bearing, commitment-bearing, incident-affected, retried, unclosed, or otherwise nonconforming prerequisite keeps preflight and provider contact blocked.

## Ten separately owned gates

1. Actual Phase 18 authorization separately satisfied.
2. Authorization reference, scope, and expiry verified.
3. Accountable sender and independent approvers verified.
4. Exact message, disclosures, and no-commitment language frozen.
5. Recipient role, official channel, and authenticity verified.
6. Data minimization, privacy, security, and recordkeeping verified.
7. Window, expiry, revocation, stop, incident, and no-retry controls bound.
8. Response disposition, quarantine, closeout, and no-intake verified.
9. No recommendation, selection, contract, account, or release verified.
10. Contact remains a separate action-time decision.

Every gate starts incomplete. Completing the design cannot create the evidence required by a gate, satisfy actual Phase 18 authorization, approve or authenticate a person, approve a message or channel, identify a recipient, open a contact window, send anything, receive or admit a response, or create standing authority.
