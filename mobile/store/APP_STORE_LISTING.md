## Description

Plan and manage hotel stays with iRatePilot.

Search approved partner properties, review available rooms, send booking requests, and follow every reservation from one account. When a hotel approves your request, complete eligible payments through Stripe’s secure checkout and see the authoritative status in Trips.

Features:

- Search approved partner hotels and rooms
- Send booking requests with stay dates and guest details
- Track pending, confirmed, cancelled, and refunded reservations
- Complete eligible card payments through Stripe
- Receive optional booking-status notifications
- Open reservation updates directly in the app
- Access privacy, terms, and support information

Availability, approval, payment, cancellation, and refund terms depend on the selected property and reservation. Card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored in the iRatePilot mobile app.
