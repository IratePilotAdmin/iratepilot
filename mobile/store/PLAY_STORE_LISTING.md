## Full description

Find and manage your next hotel stay with iRatePilot.

Explore approved partner properties, review available rooms, and send a booking request with your dates and guest count. Follow hotel decisions in Trips and complete eligible approved payments through Stripe’s secure checkout.

Use iRatePilot to:

- Search approved partner hotel inventory
- Review property and room information
- Submit hotel booking requests
- Track reservation and payment status
- Receive optional booking updates
- Open reservation notifications directly in the app
- Review cancellations and refunds

Card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored in the iRatePilot mobile app. Property availability, approval, cancellation, and refund conditions vary by reservation.
